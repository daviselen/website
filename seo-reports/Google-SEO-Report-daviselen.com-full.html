2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 62/100

What Works

  • HTTPS enforced; http:// and www. both 301 to the canonical https://daviselen.com/ apex
  • Self-referencing canonical tags present on every page checked
  • Genuine 404 status returned for non-existent URLs (verified with and without trailing slash)
  • Yoast sitemap index is valid and segmented: post, page, project, category, author
  • Fastly CDN in front of Flywheel; cached TTFB measured at 0.03s
  • No AI crawler blocks - GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and CCBot are all permitted, which is the correct default

Findings

robots.txt omits the Sitemap directive High

No 'Sitemap:' line anywhere in robots.txt, so crawlers must discover sitemap_index.xml by convention alone.

Recommendation: Add 'Sitemap: https://daviselen.com/sitemap_index.xml' to robots.txt.

'Disallow: /*?' blocks every query-string URL and contradicts the site's own WebSite schema High

The wildcard blocks ?s= search results and would block any future paginated or filtered URL. Meanwhile the WebSite JSON-LD declares a SearchAction targeting https://daviselen.com/?s={search_term_string}, a URL pattern robots.txt forbids.

Recommendation: Replace the blanket wildcard with disallow rules for specific tracking parameters, or remove it and control indexation with canonical tags.

'Crawl-delay: 3' throttles Bing for no benefit Medium

Google ignores crawl-delay entirely; Bing honours it, so this only slows Bing discovery.

Recommendation: Delete the Crawl-delay line.

Cold-cache TTFB reaches 4.7 seconds on a primary service page Medium

Measured TTFB: /capabilities/advertising 4.70s cold, /capabilities/digital 1.36s, /about 0.94s, / 0.03s on a Fastly HIT. Origin response is the variable, not the CDN.

Recommendation: Investigate origin PHP/plugin time on capabilities templates and extend Fastly cache TTL so these pages stay warm.

Security headers are nearly absent Medium

Only 'referrer-policy: no-referrer-when-downgrade' is set. No HSTS, no Content-Security-Policy, no X-Frame-Options, no Permissions-Policy.

Recommendation: Add Strict-Transport-Security, a baseline CSP, X-Frame-Options and Permissions-Policy at the Flywheel/Fastly layer. Not a direct ranking factor, but a trust and E-E-A-T signal.

2.2 Content Quality (E-E-A-T)

Score: 35/100

What Works

  • Genuine experience signals exist in the copy: 75+ years in business, Toyota for 5 decades, McDonald's for 6, $18 billion in annual retail sales
  • Real third-party validation is linked out: NBA.com Lakers, Innovid case study, One Club award, PadSquad case study
  • /privacy-policy is substantive at 7,909 words

Findings

All 9 capabilities pages are thin High

Word counts: advertising 535, digital 344, multicultural 331, activations 331, packaging 326, branding 309, pr-social 311, experiential 268, who 228. These are the site's commercial pages and cannot compete for '[service] agency los angeles' style queries at this depth.

Recommendation: Expand each to 800+ words with named client work, campaign results, process detail and team credentials.

Homepage carries only 194 rendered words High

Playwright body innerText word count is 194 against a 171 KB HTML payload. The page is almost entirely video and styling.

Recommendation: Add substantive positioning copy that states who the agency serves, in which categories, with what proof.

WordPress demo content is publicly live Critical

/sample-page, /hello-world and /test all return 200 and sit in the sitemap.

Recommendation: Delete all three in WordPress. Do not noindex - delete, then confirm 404/410 and resubmit the sitemap.

The only page with real depth is the legal boilerplate Medium

/privacy-policy at 7,909 words dwarfs every commercial page on the site, and it carries two <h1> elements and no meta description.

Recommendation: Fix the duplicate H1, add a meta description, and treat the depth gap as the content roadmap's starting signal.

Two near-empty pages sit in the sitemap Medium

/capabilities-deck is 124 words and /davis-elen-advertising-retail-presence-map is 19 words.

Recommendation: Either build these out or noindex them and drop them from the sitemap.

2.3 On-Page SEO

Score: 30/100

What Works

  • Title tags are present and unique on all 30 pages, mostly within the 28-72 character range
  • Canonical URLs are correct sitewide
  • Primary navigation exposes real crawlable <a href> links to /work, /about, /careers and /contact

Findings

25 of 30 pages have no <h1>; the homepage has no heading of any level Critical

Only /about (1), /about-leadership (1) and /privacy-policy (2, a duplicate) contain an H1. The rendered homepage DOM returns headingsTotal: 0 across h1 through h6. Search engines and LLM retrieval pipelines both chunk pages by heading hierarchy; with none present, each page is one undifferentiated blob. This single defect simultaneously depresses On-Page, Content Quality and AI Search Readiness.

Recommendation: Add one semantic <h1> per page and convert styled section divs into <h2>/<h3>. Fix the duplicate H1 on /privacy-policy.

17 pages have no meta description High

Affected: all 9 /capabilities/*, /careers, /privacy-policy, /toyota-ugc, /lively-sim, /capabilities-deck, /test, /davis-elen-advertising-retail-presence-map, and 3 of 4 position pages.

Recommendation: Write unique 135-160 character descriptions for each.

6 pages ship the literal Yoast placeholder description High

/contact, /about-leadership, /game-on, /smart-final-alex, /de-tuesdays and /position-project-manager all serve: 'Add a description about your page or projects. Should be around 135 to 160 characters.'

Recommendation: Replace all six immediately - this text is visible in SERP snippets.

Only 3 of 30 pages have a real meta description High

/ (130 chars), /work (158 chars) and /about (165 chars, 5 over the practical truncation limit).

Recommendation: Trim /about to under 160 and treat these three as the template for the other 27.

Homepage does not link to any capabilities page Medium

All 9 /capabilities/* URLs are linked only from /work. /about and /careers link to none of them. This concentrates all capability-page authority behind a single hub.

Recommendation: Add contextual links to capabilities pages from the homepage and from /about.

og:title drops the brand differentiator Low

og:title is 'Davis Elen Advertising' while the <title> is 'Davis Elen Advertising - The oldest independent agency on the West Coast'. Social shares lose the positioning line.

Recommendation: Align og:title and twitter:title with the full title tag.

2.4 Schema / Structured Data

Score: 45/100

What Works

  • Valid Yoast @graph on every page: WebPage, BreadcrumbList, WebSite, Organization
  • Organization includes logo as a proper ImageObject with dimensions and caption
  • sameAs covers Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn
  • inLanguage set to en-US consistently

Findings

Organization is missing every local and entity property that matters High

No address, no telephone, no foundingDate, no numberOfEmployees, and the generic Organization type rather than the AdvertisingAgency subtype. The site publicly states 75+ years in business and lists six offices, none of it marked up.

Recommendation: Upgrade to @type AdvertisingAgency with postalAddress, telephone +1-213-688-7000, foundingDate and numberOfEmployees.

Four open job postings carry no JobPosting schema High

/position-copywriter, /position-art-director, /position-production-designer and /position-project-manager return only the generic Yoast graph. Without JobPosting they are ineligible for Google Jobs.

Recommendation: Add JobPosting to all four with title, description, datePosted, validThrough, hiringOrganization, jobLocation and baseSalary where disclosable.

Six office locations have no LocalBusiness markup High

Los Angeles HQ, San Diego, Seattle, Arlington, Kansas City and Denver appear on /contact as plain unmarked text.

Recommendation: Add a LocalBusiness entity per office. Six locations is well under the 30-page location quality gate, so dedicated location pages are safe to build - provided each carries genuinely local content rather than a city-swapped template.

Leadership page has no Person schema Medium

/about-leadership renders 78 images (headshots) with no Person entities, so no author or executive entity graph exists.

Recommendation: Add Person schema per executive with name, jobTitle, image and worksFor pointing at the Organization @id.

BreadcrumbList contains a single 'Home' item on every page Low

Yoast default, never configured, so no page communicates its position in the hierarchy.

Recommendation: Configure breadcrumb depth in Yoast so /capabilities/* pages emit Home > Capabilities > [Service].

2.5 Performance (Core Web Vitals)

Score: 35/100

What Works

  • LCP measured at 1,524 ms, inside the 2.5s 'good' threshold
  • FCP at 1,188 ms
  • Light DOM: 588 nodes on the homepage
  • Only one long task recorded during load

Findings

CLS of 0.58 against a 0.10 'good' threshold Critical

Two layout shifts with no recent input. Root causes: images served without width/height attributes, and the hero video replacing its placeholder after load.

Recommendation: Set intrinsic width/height on every image and reserve an aspect-ratio box for the hero video container.

Homepage transfers 30.1 MB Critical

Breakdown: video 25,474 KB (hompageLoop-6.mp4 alone), CSS 2,530 KB across 12 files, images 1,272 KB, scripts 788 KB. DE4-TYT-Thumb-Long.jpg is 1,700 KB by itself.

Recommendation: Compress and re-encode the hero video, or poster-frame it and load on interaction. Target under 3 MB total.

2.5 MB of CSS across 12 separate files High

Twelve render-blocking stylesheets totalling more than the entire recommended page budget.

Recommendation: Concatenate, purge unused rules and inline critical CSS.

INP not measurable without field data Info

INP requires real user interaction data from CrUX. No Google API key is configured, so no field measurement was possible in this run.

Recommendation: Configure a GOOGLE_API_KEY and re-run to capture CrUX p75 INP, LCP and CLS with 25 weeks of history.

2.6 AI Search Readiness (GEO)

Score: 30/100

What Works

  • No AI crawler is blocked in robots.txt - GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot and Google-Extended all have access
  • Organization entity with sameAs gives AI systems a resolvable brand identity
  • External third-party citations (Innovid, One Club, NBA.com, PadSquad) corroborate claims made on-site

Findings

No heading structure means no citable passages Critical

AI retrieval systems extract and cite passage-level chunks bounded by headings. With zero headings sitewide, there is nothing for a retrieval pipeline to isolate and quote.

Recommendation: Fixing the heading defect is the single highest-leverage GEO action available; every other GEO improvement depends on it.

Content depth is below the citation threshold High

194-535 words per page gives AI systems almost nothing quotable about services, process or results.

Recommendation: Pair the content expansion with explicit, self-contained factual statements that read correctly when quoted out of context.

Entity data is incomplete for AI knowledge extraction Medium

No address, telephone, founding date or employee count in structured data, so AI systems cannot confidently resolve the firm's size, age or locations.

Recommendation: Complete the Organization/AdvertisingAgency entity as described in the Schema findings.

No llms.txt file present Low

https://daviselen.com/llms.txt returns 404. Google Search ignores llms.txt entirely; treatment by ChatGPT and Perplexity is unconfirmed.

Recommendation: Low cost, unproven benefit. Backlog item, not a priority - do not displace heading or content work for it.

2.7 Images

Score: 45/100

What Works

  • Homepage, /about, /work, /careers and the position pages have complete alt coverage
  • Logo delivered as SVG
  • Only one homepage image lacks alt text

Findings

9 images missing alt text on every capabilities page Medium

Identical count of 9 across all 9 /capabilities/* pages, indicating a single shared template component is at fault.

Recommendation: Fix the one component - it corrects 81 image instances across 9 pages.

Zero lazy loading sitewide Medium

Playwright reports lazyImgs: 0. Every below-fold image loads eagerly, including 78 headshots on /about-leadership and 71 images on /about.

Recommendation: Add loading=lazy to all below-fold images.

No modern image formats in use Medium

Only jpg, png and svg are served. No WebP or AVIF anywhere. DE4-TYT-Thumb-Long.jpg is 1,700 KB and would be roughly 150 KB as WebP.

Recommendation: Convert raster images to WebP with jpg/png fallback via <picture>.

Images lack intrinsic dimensions Medium

Directly contributes to the 0.58 CLS finding.

Recommendation: Emit width and height attributes from the WordPress media pipeline.

2.8 DE5 Rebuild Risk

Score: 40/100

What Works

  • Modern stack in the local repository: React 18, Vite, Tailwind, GSAP, React Router 7
  • Playwright visual regression tests already wired up in the project
  • Rebuild is pre-launch, so SEO defects can be prevented rather than retrofitted

Findings

Client-rendered SPA with no SSR or prerender step High

The local repository (davis-elen-website 5.1.2-alpha) is a React Router SPA built with Vite. Without server-side rendering or a prerender step, the initial HTML response is an empty shell. This is a harder version of the current site's problem, not a fix for it.

Recommendation: Add SSR or static prerendering before launch, and verify that view-source (not just the rendered DOM) contains titles, meta descriptions, headings and body copy.

Heading semantics must be enforced in components High

The live site's zero-heading defect comes from styling divs as display type. The same pattern in React components would carry the defect straight through the rebuild.

Recommendation: Add a lint rule or review gate requiring semantic h1-h6 for all display type, and assert one H1 per route in the existing Playwright suite.

Per-route meta tags need explicit handling Medium

React Router does not manage document head by default, so per-route titles and descriptions must be wired deliberately.

Recommendation: Implement per-route head management and port the corrected meta descriptions from the DE4 fixes rather than re-authoring them.
